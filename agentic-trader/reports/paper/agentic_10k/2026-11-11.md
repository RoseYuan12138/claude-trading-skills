## 3. The trade

**BUY 1 x QQQ 2027-08-20 655 call, limit at the mid (model $92.66; paper fill $93.12 = model +
0.5% half spread).** Premium $9,312 = **93% of the account** (the most this position can lose);
cash left $688. Delta 0.68 -> about **4.7x QQQ exposure**. Breakeven at expiry: QQQ 748.12 (+8.4%).

Rule (chosen after the backtest below): two-index LEAPS trend calls with a **strike ladder at a
95% premium budget**: buy the deepest-in-the-money strike among 15% / 10% / 5% ITM / ATM of which
one contract (plus 0.5% half spread) fits within 95% of equity. Today that is the 5% ITM 655C.
As equity grows past ~$15k the ladder moves back to the researched 15% ITM strike, and leverage
falls from ~4.7x toward ~3.5x.

## 4. Exit plan

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| SPY **or** QQQ closes below its 200-day SMA (QQQ SMA 681.71 today, rising ~0.3/day) | Sell at the next open, limit near the mid; hold cash (T-bills). Model: at QQQ 681 the account is ~$9,400 (-6%) |
| Both back above | Re-enter with the ladder at the current equity |
| 60 days or fewer to expiry (on/after **2027-06-21**) while both above | Roll: sell, buy a new ~270-day call by the ladder |
| Equity >= **$100,000** (marked at the model) | Sell everything, stop trading for good ("收手") |
| Equity < **$3,000** | Sell everything, stop and report |
| Equity too small to buy one ATM contract within 95% (today: below ~$7,550) when a re-entry is due | Stay in cash and report: the account is "stuck" (the backtest's soft-failure mode) |

No percentage stop on the option itself: the regime exit is the stop. Stops and trailing stops
were tested earlier and hurt this strategy.

Chosen rule, by start year: reached $100k from every start except 2004, 2005 and 2011 (stuck in

Checks of the prior findings: confirmed. TQQQ and SOXL calls lose to QQQ calls by a wide margin
and are frequently wiped out; ATM QQQ calls reach the goal fastest but with 87-91% worst
drawdowns and 2-3 ruins; the 30% version is far too slow to turn $10k into $100k.

**Odds of $10k -> $100k:** roughly **50-65% within 10 years** (14/22 rolling starts; the prior
block bootstrap gave 46-53%), **~5-15% within 5 years** (1/22 here, 9-17% in the bootstrap), and
**~10-15% chance of failing first** (3/22 stuck here; 7-14% ruin in the bootstrap). Expect a
50-70% drawdown along the way.

## 6. Main ways this blows up

1. **Whipsaw lock-out.** QQQ is only 1.3% above its SMA. A string of small regime exits (each
   -5% to -15% of the account at 4-5x leverage) can push equity below the cost of one ATM
   contract (~$7.5k), after which the account cannot re-enter. This is how 2004, 2005 and 2011
   failed.
2. **Overnight crash before the exit.** A -15 to -20% gap (1987, 2020-style) would cost about
   35-45% of the account before the regime exit can act.
3. **Model vs real quotes.** Real QQQ LEAPS spreads and skew may be worse than the model; at a
   2.5% half spread the research CAGR fell by half. The journal records model prices only until
   real quotes are added with `--quote`.
4. **The Nasdaq-100 drift fades.** The edge is a leveraged bet on QQQ's long-run trend; a long
   sideways market (2000-2013 style) means many whipsaws and a slow or failed path to $100k.
